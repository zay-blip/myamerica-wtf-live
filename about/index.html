The sphere

MyAmerica.WTF is the living map of this project. Version 0.1 is the spine: 5 zones, 50 states, 50 capitals, 100 cities, and 12 shared topic nodes. Small towns, historic buildings, and local knowledge can land on that geography without breaking it. Color on the map is regional, not political. Later layers mark acquired properties, verified records, and restorations.
